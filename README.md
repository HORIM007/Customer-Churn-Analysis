Customer Churn Analysis Using SQL and Power BI

Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn in a telecommunications company to identify customer segments with high churn rates and provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

Objectives

- Calculate the overall customer churn rate.
- Analyze churn by contract type and customer tenure.
- Investigate churn by internet service, payment method, and technical support.
- Develop data-driven recommendations for customer retention.

Tools Used

- SQL (MySQL): Data analysis, aggregations, and churn rate calculations.
- Power BI: Dashboard development and data visualization.

Key Metrics

- Total Customers: 7,032
- Churned Customers: 1,869
- Retained Customers: 5,163
- Overall Churn Rate: 26.58%

Key Business Insights

1. Customer Tenure: Customers in the 0–12 month group had the highest churn rate at 47.68%.
2. Contract Type: Month-to-month customers had a churn rate of 42.71%, compared with 11.28% for one-year contracts and 2.85% for two-year contracts.
3. Internet Service: Fiber-optic customers had a churn rate of 41.89%.
4. Payment Method: Electronic-check customers had the highest churn rate at 45.29%.
5. Technical Support: Customers without technical support had a churn rate of 41.65%.

Recommendations

- Strengthen onboarding and engagement during customers' first year.
- Investigate incentives for longer-term contracts.
- Review fiber-optic service quality, pricing, and customer complaints.
- Investigate the reasons for higher churn among electronic-check customers.
- Improve access to technical support and proactive issue resolution.

Conclusion

The analysis highlights customer segments that may require targeted retention efforts. These findings can help the business investigate churn drivers and develop strategies to improve customer satisfaction and retention.

Project Files

The repository contains SQL queries for analyzing churn by contract type, tenure, internet service, payment method, and technical support, alongside overall churn metrics.

Disclaimer

The findings describe patterns in the dataset and do not establish causation. Further investigation is needed to validate the underlying reasons for customer churn.
